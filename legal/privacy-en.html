2. Summary
You can use Gezi without an account. Your likes, routes and settings then stay on your phone only and are not sent to us. An account is needed only for syncing between devices, a profile and (later) reviews. Your location is not stored on our servers. Your travel photos are never uploaded.

3. Data that stays on your phone (not sent to us)
Travel journal photos, notes, collages and PDF summaries (location data is removed from photos when added; with auto-match on, the location is read once on the phone only to suggest a place), liked/skipped places, recently viewed places, filters, pace, route settings and saved routes, the "for someone else" route label (route name and person's name), language and unit settings, downloaded city packs.

4. Data stored on the server if you create an account, and why
- Account: email address (email sign-up) or the account identifier provided by Apple/Google; passwords only as a hash. Purpose: sign-in. With Sign in with Apple we do not request your name or email.
- Profile: username, optional short bio and profile photo, whether the profile is private/public and what you share. Profiles are PRIVATE by default. Before upload the profile photo is resized on your phone and all metadata (EXIF), including location, is removed.
- Consent records: "16 or older" confirmation, accepted version and time of the terms and this policy, your analytics choice. Purpose: proving legal compliance.
- Sync: IDs of liked places, saved routes (order of places, dates and settings; no location history), cities and countries with routes (date only).
- Travel journal: only "visited" marks (place + date) and earned badges. Photos and notes are never uploaded.
- Reviews: rating, text, language and date. Reviews are public and shown with your username only; your email is never shown. Your "helpful" marks and blocked users are visible only to you.
- Reports and moderation: content you report, the reason and an optional note; notices explaining why your content was hidden/removed. Reported users are not told who reported them. Legal basis: legal obligation (EU Digital Services Act) and legitimate interest.
Legal basis: performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for consent records legal obligation and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(c), (f) GDPR).

5. Public profile
If you make your profile public, others see only your username, bio, profile photo and the items you explicitly choose to share (likes, trip history, visited cities). Your email address is never shown.

6. Location and photos
- Location is used only when you tap "Find my city" or "Sort by distance" — once, on your phone, never sent or stored. No background location tracking.
- Address search (accommodation) uses your phone's own map service (Apple or Google); the search text goes to that service.
- Photo access is requested only when choosing a profile photo; iOS "limited access" is supported.
